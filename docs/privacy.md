# KickIQ Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

## The short version
KickIQ films a kick, measures it on your phone, and asks an AI coach to score it. Your videos stay on your phone. We never sell your data, and we never show your name or face to other players.

## What we collect
- Your date of birth, to check you are 13 or older. Only the birth year leaves your device.
- Your quiz answers: position, level, biggest weakness, goal, training frequency.
- Your Kick Ratings, the coaching text, and the body-position measurements for each kick.
- A first name, only if you add one for the weekly leaderboard.

## What leaves your phone
Six still images from your clip (approach, plant, contact, follow-through, ball flight, finish) and the measurement numbers are sent to our analysis service and to Google Gemini to produce your rating. They are not kept after the rating is returned. The full video never uploads. A score-card image is uploaded only if you choose to share it.

## Where your data lives
Ratings and profile answers are stored on your phone and, when you are online, in your private account on Supabase (hosted in the United States). Each account is anonymous: no email, no password, no phone number.

## Payments
Subscriptions are handled by Apple, Google and RevenueCat. We see an anonymous subscriber ID and your plan, never your card details.

## Analytics
We use PostHog to count things like how many kicks are rated and where players drop off in onboarding. This is linked to a random ID, not to your name.

## Teens and parents
KickIQ is for players aged 13 and over. There are no public profiles, no chat, and no messaging between players. Leaderboards show a first name and initial only. Parents can ask us to delete an account at any time by emailing support@kickiq.app.

## Delete your data
Settings → Delete my data removes everything from your phone and our servers immediately. You can also email support@kickiq.app.

## Not medical advice
KickIQ gives technique coaching only. It does not diagnose, prevent or treat injuries. If something hurts, stop and talk to a coach, parent or doctor.

## Changes
If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

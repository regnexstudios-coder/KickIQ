# KickIQ Terms of Use

_Last updated: October 24, 2026_

## Using KickIQ
You must be 13 or older. Use the app for your own training. Do not film other people without their permission, and do not upload clips that are not of a soccer kick.

## Subscriptions
KickIQ Pro is a subscription with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Your App Store or Google Play account is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. Plans renew automatically until cancelled in your store account settings. Prices are shown in the app before you subscribe.

| Plan | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | $3.99 / week | Flexible |

## Family plan
The Family plan covers up to five players in one family sharing group through the App Store or Google Play family features.

## Referral weeks
Referral rewards are seven days of Pro per successful referral, are not transferable, and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## The rating is advice, not a promise
Kick Ratings and drills are generated by software and an AI model. They are estimates to help you train and can be wrong. Train sensibly, with proper warm-up and supervision where appropriate.

## Your content
Your videos belong to you and stay on your device. By sharing a score card you allow the person you share it with to see your rating.

## Fair play
Do not try to break the app, cheat leaderboards, or use KickIQ for anything unlawful. We may remove leaderboard entries or close accounts that do.
